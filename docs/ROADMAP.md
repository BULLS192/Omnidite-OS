## Portfolio guardrails
- Tier 1 gets capacity before Tier 2 and Tier 3.
- Any Tier 1 milestone due within 14 days that is red/blocked can take over Saturday Tier 2 time.
- Tier 3 receives only a controlled Sunday incubation slot unless promoted.
- A milestone is complete only when its exit criterion is verified.
- Sunday morning is the formal portfolio review and recovery window.
- Six-month closeout assigns every project: **Scale / Maintain / Incubate / Archive**.
